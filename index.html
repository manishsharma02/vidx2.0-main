Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about competing, earning XP, climbing campus rankings, and daily discipline on VidX.

Your campus rank is calculated in real-time based on total XP earned from solved problems, active streak bonuses, and live battle victories. You can filter the rankings by your specific college department/branch, graduation year (e.g. 2024–2028), or compare against the entire student cohort.
The Daily Sadhana problem resets every night at midnight (12:00 AM IST). Solving the problem before midnight preserves your daily streak and awards bonus XP with a streak multiplier. Missing a day resets your streak counter to zero, making consistency your biggest competitive edge.
Code Battles are real-time competitive matches. You can join scheduled 1v1 clashes against peers or create private battle rooms with a custom room code to challenge classmates. Both players receive the same problem simultaneously with synchronized timers and automated instant judging.
